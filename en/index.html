FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does rental cost?+

It depends on the class and duration. Prices start at $15 per day. The longer the rental, the cheaper the day, and some models have discounts.

What is the deposit for?+

It is the amount you are liable for that insurance does not cover. The deposit is required, but extra insurance can reduce it.

Can I pick up and return the car at my address?+

Yes, we deliver almost anywhere in Ukraine. Delivery within Kyiv is free for rentals of several days.

How do I check if a car is free on my dates?+

Register in your personal account and press “Order a car”, or message a manager on Telegram, Viber or WhatsApp.

Can I travel abroad?+

Yes, rentals with trips abroad are available. Please confirm the terms with a manager before booking.
